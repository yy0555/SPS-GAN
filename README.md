# Structured Phase-Space GAN

Official implementation of **Discovering Phase Space Structure in Learned Hamiltonian Systems** (Yang, Liu, Bansal, Allen-Blanchette), published at NeurIPS 2026 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23003). SPS-GAN is a conditional generative model with a port-Hamiltonian latent phase space that generates trajectories and videos of conservative, dissipative, and forced systems while identifying their effective degrees of freedom and interpretable mechanical coordinates.
